How matching works
  • A charge matches an order when the amount is the same to the cent and the charge date falls inside the day window above. Matches where the store name also shows up in the bank description rank first.
  • If the amount is off by less than the allowed percentage (shipping, tax, a partial refund) and the store name matches, the pair goes to Needs a look.
  • Split shipments are handled: one order charged as two or three separate charges, or two or three orders from the same store charged together.
  • Bank names like AMZN Mktp, WM Supercenter and TGT are recognized as Amazon, Walmart and Target.
  • Click Not a match to break a pairing and re-run. Click Ignore merchant on everyday charges (groceries, gas) to hide them; this is remembered in this browser.
  • Orders placed after your statement ends are labelled as such, since those charges may not have posted yet.
